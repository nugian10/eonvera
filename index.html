Article I — The Eon Engine

One line of time, grown into an era.

Each cell looks at itself and its two neighbours, then decides to live or die on the next line. 8 neighbour patterns, 8 answers — that is the entire law. Change the answers below and watch a new era write itself.

Why Rule 150?

Of the 256 possible laws, most die instantly or repeat in boring loops. Eonvera only takes the laws that produce structures worth looking at — symmetric, controllably chaotic, or capable of universal computation like Rule 110.

Four ways an era grows

  • Fractal — endless Sierpinski triangles (Rule 90 / 150)
  • Chaos — never repeats, never the same (Rule 30)
  • Complex — moving structures that can compute anything (Rule 110)
  • Still — frozen into geometric textiles (pure Rule 184)
Eon + Vera. Eon = the longest span of time. Vera = true. Every Eonvera is a record of time that cannot be forged — its seed is locked into the block in which it was minted.
Article II — Mint

Only agents may mint.

The contract accepts exactly one kind of caller: a Binance Agentic Wallet holding a claimed Agent ID. 🔒 The Agent ID gate — how the check worksAgent-only

Minting is gated on-chain, not by this page. When mint() is called, the contract runs three checks in order — and reverts if any one fails:

  1. Caller is a Binance Agentic Wallet. The call must come from the agentic wallet you control in the Binance Wallet app. Calls routed through a regular wallet app are turned away.
  2. That wallet holds a claimed Agent ID. The Agent ID you claimed under Binance Wallet → Agentic Wallet (issued via SPACE ID) is looked up against the caller. An agentic wallet that never claimed its Agent ID is rejected.
  3. Caller is not another contract. Smart-contract callers are refused outright, so bots and proxy contracts cannot mint around the gate.

In short: ordinary wallets and other contracts are turned away — only an agent acting through its Binance Agentic Wallet, with its Agent ID claimed, can mint an Eonvera.

Notes — FAQ

Questions that come up often.

Why can only agents mint?

Eonvera is about laws that run without human hands, so it is minted by software acting on its own. The contract requires a Binance Agentic Wallet holding an Agent ID claimed in the Binance Wallet app (issued via SPACE ID). Connect a regular wallet instead and there is nothing to mint with — the call never gets past the gate.

What happens if I mint from a regular wallet or another contract?

The transaction reverts. The contract checks that the caller is a Binance Agentic Wallet, that the wallet holds a claimed Agent ID, and that the caller is not another smart contract. Ordinary wallets and contract callers fail those checks and are turned away — no Eonvera is minted and no price is taken, though the failed transaction still costs its network gas.

Where do I claim an Agent ID?

In the Binance Wallet app: open your Agentic Wallet and claim the Agent ID shown there. That claim is what the Eonvera contract verifies on-chain when your agent calls mint().

What decides my work?

A seed made from the block and your address at mint. That seed picks the form, the law, the starting row, and the palette. The same seed always draws the same work.

Where does the work live?

The seed is stored on BNB Chain. The drawing code is open and runs on this page, so anyone can redraw any Eonvera from its number.

What do I get?

A work of art and a way to study it — its seed and ownership recorded on-chain. Nothing beyond that is promised.

What is the mint price?

The mint price is set on-chain at 0.0003 BNB per work, read directly from the contract via currentPrice() at mint — your agent reads it and shows it in your wallet before you sign.

When does the market open?

After the genesis mint begins — a work can only be listed, offered on, or sold once it has been minted and has an owner. Until then the market section shows live engine previews of the works, with no prices, because no real listings exist yet.

What is on the roadmap?

Five phases: the Eon Engine (live now), contract deployment on BNB Chain, the agent-only mint, the market opening, and finally the catalogue & curatorial programme. Eonvera ships in phases rather than on promised dates — see the roadmap for the current status of each phase.
